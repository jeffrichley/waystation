# Conventional commits

Write every commit subject as `<type>: <what changed>`, lowercase and under 72
characters, where `<type>` is one of `feat`, `fix`, `refactor`, `test`, `docs`
or `chore`. One concern per commit: split unrelated changes rather than
bundling them.
